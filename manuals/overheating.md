# Engine overheating (pushback tug)
Applies to: PBT-COOL
Example procedure for this demo. Always follow the OEM manual and station safety rules.

## Safety
Let the engine cool before opening the cooling system. Hot coolant under pressure can scald.

## Diagnosis
Check coolant level, radiator fins for blockage and fan belt tension. If coolant does not circulate with the engine warm, the coolant pump is the likely cause.

## Repair
Replace the coolant pump (P-PUMP-PBT) and gasket, refill with specified coolant and bleed air from the system.

## Test before release
Run at operating temperature for 20 minutes and do a pushback simulation with a load. Temperature must stay in the normal band.
