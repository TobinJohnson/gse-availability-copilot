# Tyre damaged (small GSE)
Applies to: PS-TYRE, BT-TYRE
Example procedure for this demo. Always follow the OEM manual and station safety rules.

## Safety
Jack only at the marked points and support the unit on stands before removing a wheel. FOD check the area afterwards.

## Diagnosis
Look for cuts, chunking or flat spots. Check the wheel rim for cracks.

## Repair
Replace the solid tyre (P-TYRE-S). Torque wheel nuts to the OEM value.

## Test before release
Drive a short test loop and re-check wheel nut torque. Record the failure code and part used.
