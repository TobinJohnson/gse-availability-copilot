# Hydraulic leak (lift, conveyor or scissor system)
Applies to: BL-HYD, PS-LIFT, CHL-HYD, PBT-HOSE
Example procedure for this demo. Always follow the OEM manual and station safety rules.

## Safety
Lower the platform or conveyor fully and fit the mechanical safety prop. Switch off, remove the key and tag the unit out of service. Wear gloves and eye protection; hydraulic oil under pressure can penetrate skin.

## Diagnosis
Clean the area and run the lift once to find the leak point. Seepage at the cylinder rod seal means a seal kit; a split or wet hose means a hose replacement. Check the oil level and look for metal particles in the oil.

## Repair
Release system pressure. For a rod seal: remove the cylinder, replace the seal kit (P-SEAL-BL, P-SEAL-PS or P-SEAL-CHL by unit type) and refit. For a burst hose on a pushback tug: replace the hose (P-HOSE-PBT) and torque fittings to OEM values. Top up with the specified oil.

## Test before release
Cycle the lift five times under no load, then once at rated load. Check for drift over 10 minutes and for any leak. Record the failure code and part used on the work order.
