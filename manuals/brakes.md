# Brake wear (pushback tug, baggage tractor)
Applies to: PBT-BRK, BT-BRK
Example procedure for this demo. Always follow the OEM manual and station safety rules.

## Safety
Park on level ground, chock the wheels and take the unit out of service. Brakes are safety-critical: a supervisor signs off the repair.

## Diagnosis
Measure pad or lining thickness against the OEM minimum. Check for fluid leaks at the calipers and test the parking brake hold.

## Repair
Replace the brake set (P-BRK-PBT for pushback tugs, P-BRK-BT for baggage tractors) on both sides of the axle. Bleed the system if it was opened.

## Test before release
Do a static brake test and a low-speed stopping test on the ramp test lane. Supervisor signs the work order before release.
