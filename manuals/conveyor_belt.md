# Conveyor belt torn or slipping (belt loader)
Applies to: BL-BELT
Example procedure for this demo. Always follow the OEM manual and station safety rules.

## Safety
Switch off, remove the key and isolate the conveyor drive. Keep hands clear of rollers.

## Diagnosis
Inspect the belt along its full length for tears, frayed edges and worn lacing. If it slips, check belt tension and the drive roller lagging before replacing the belt.

## Repair
Slacken the tension rollers, remove the old belt and fit the new conveyor belt (P-BELT-BL). Align and tension it to the OEM marks.

## Test before release
Run the conveyor both directions empty for two minutes, then with a test bag. The belt must track centrally without slipping. Record the failure code and part used.
